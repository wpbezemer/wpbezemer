# Hey, I'm Bunny 👋

> *"If it's dumb and it doesn't know it — I'll teach it."*

I'm a Dutch IoT enthusiast who genuinely believes that most devices out there are just one firmware flash away from being useful. Making dumb things smarter is not a job for me — it's a passion, a Saturday afternoon, and sometimes a rabbit hole that ends at 2am with a soldering iron in one hand and a datasheet in the other.

---

## What drives me

- **IoT is my hobby and my happy place.** From Zigbee sensors to ESP chips buried in random appliances, if it has electricity running through it, I'm curious about what it *could* do.
- **Automation nerd.** If something can be automated, I want to know why it hasn't been yet — and then do it anyway.
- **Dumb devices deserve better.** Off-the-shelf gadgets with locked-down firmware and zero local control are an invitation, not a barrier. Cloudcutter, ESPHome, and a healthy curiosity go a long way.
- **Home Assistant is home base.** Most of what I build ends up there: custom Lovelace cards, ESPHome configs, automations, and integrations.
